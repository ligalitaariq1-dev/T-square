Our Origin: The Drawing Board

"We don't just build, code, or repair; we engineer solutions with accuracy."

When founding this company, the biggest challenge was finding a single name that could encompass a diverse multi-disciplinary skill set spanning software development, advanced home automation, and mechanical engineering. Initial ideas like 'Modern Development' felt too restrictive—they captured the digital side but completely left out the hands-on engineering, gears, and hardware logic.

That is how T-Square was born. A T-square is the ultimate foundational tool used in technical drawing, architecture, and structural design to establish absolute precision and perfect alignment. For us, it serves as the perfect bridge.

Whether we are aligning pixels on a website, structuring programmatic logic for a smart home automation system, calibrating mechanical engine parts, or teaching the foundational principles of Mathematics, Physics, and Technical Drawing—everything we do operates on the exact same standard of scientific, geometric precision.
